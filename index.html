Teaching, not just building

In 2024, I designed and delivered an ICT skills programme for 26 inmates at Pietermaritzburg Medium A Correctional Centre, working with staff to align it with rehabilitation goals.

That work led to RoadToRedemption, a team project helping ex-offenders find work, build digital skills and reach mentors.
